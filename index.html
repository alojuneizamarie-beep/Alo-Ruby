How we met

Ruby and I met during the "Freshmen Got Talent" event at our 2026 Week of Welcome. At first, we were both too shy to talk to each other since it was our first time interacting. However, as the days passed, we started eating lunch together every day and catching up on all the "chika" about our lives. Now, it feels as if we've known each other for years. What started as quiet, hesitant conversations quickly turned into non-stop laughter and shared secrets. From navigating our new campus routine to debriefing our daily experiences over lunch, Ruby has easily become my go-to person. It's funny how a single orientation event brought together someone who feels less like a new classmate and more like a lifelong friend.

Our Similarities
